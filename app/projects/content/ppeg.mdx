my dissertation! a peg parsing library written in rust, that supports left recursive grammars. this is achieved through storing a short history of previous parser routes to avoid infinitely recursing. this history is remembered until some progress is made on the parse, at which point the history is cleared. this really deserves a bit more writing about and maybe even a blog post, but for now, check out the repo [https://github.com/joseph-beck/ppeg](https://github.com/joseph-beck/ppeg)!
